## ✨ Features

- **🌈 3-Layer Volumetric Neon Glow**:
  1. **Ambient Bloom Layer**: Wide translucent color halo illuminating the dark background.
  2. **Mid-Tone Halo Layer**: Offset bloom generating physical tube-light diffusion.
  3. **Core Filament**: Crisp, high-contrast typography in pure saturated neon colors.
- **✨ Border Accents & Floating Hearts**:
  - Hollow neon pink heart `♡` on the left flank.
  - Floating magenta and ultraviolet hearts `♥` on the flanks, notch, and bottom tip.
  - Twinkling star specks (`✦`, `•`) and glowing contour trace along the cardioid perimeter.
- **🪟 Landscape Right-Side Docking**:
  - Opens in a wide horizontal orientation (`1040x720`) docked on the **right side** of your monitor.
  - Automatically elevates over your editor and terminal on launch.
